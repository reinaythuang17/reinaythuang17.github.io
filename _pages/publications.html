Working Papers

Public Payment Mandates and Provider Supply

(with Lauren Hoehn-Velasco , Olanrewaju Yusuff)*Under Review

[SSRN] [IZA]

Public insurance reimbursement policies shape the structure and reach of healthcare markets. In this study, we examine the 1980 federal Medicaid mandate requiring states to reimburse Certified Nurse-Midwives (CNMs), one of the first federal reforms targeting non-physician providers. We find the mandate increased midwife-attended deliveries by 1.1 percentage points in states that adopted the mandate before 1988. This 1.1 percentage point increase reflects a 69% rise in midwife deliveries, adding almost 1,300 midwife births annually per state by 1988. We also document a geographic expansion of midwife services into unserved areas and increased hospital employment. Our findings demonstrate that reimbursement mandates alter healthcare delivery by expanding provider use and reshaping the workforce.
